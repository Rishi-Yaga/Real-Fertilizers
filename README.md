$# 🌱 Real Fertilizers – Entrepreneurship & Product Development Project

## 📌 Overview
This project focuses on building a sustainable fertilizer venture by applying entrepreneurial frameworks, economic value analysis, and structured product development strategies to address agricultural productivity challenges.

---

## 🚀 Project Highlight 

- Farmers faced rising input costs and declining soil health, creating demand for cost-effective and environmentally sustainable fertilizer solutions.  
- Develop a viable fertilizer business model integrating economic feasibility, product innovation, and market strategy.  
- Conducted market research and competitor analysis, evaluated cost structures and pricing strategy, defined value proposition, designed product development roadmap, and performed break-even and ROI analysis to validate financial sustainability.  
- Developed a scalable business model demonstrating strong economic viability, competitive differentiation, and sustainable product positioning aligned with long-term agricultural impact.
